Our Story

From one man’s craft to a trusted family name.

A besan pinni made by hand became a name Malwa families have trusted for thirty years.

Lala Om Parkash Ji spent 20 years as a canteen contractor at ITI Ferozepur, known across the district for the besan pinni and mathi he made by hand.

In 1995 he began making pinni, mathi and bhujia from home in Kotkapura, with his wife Mrs. Kailash Rani Ji beside him. Together they built ॐOm Parkash Namkeens into a name families trusted for mathi, namkeen and wedding bhaji.

The range grew past 60 varieties and reached homes across Malwa. Today their sons — Raj Kumar, Rajendra Kumar and Deepak Kumar — carry the same standard forward.
