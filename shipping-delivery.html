Om Parkash Namkeens is a family-run namkeen maker based in Kotkapura, Punjab. Orders are placed as requests through WhatsApp, and delivery details are confirmed with you directly before dispatch.

Delivery areas

We deliver locally in and around Kotkapura and nearby towns. If you are outside our regular delivery area, message us on WhatsApp with your PIN code and we will let you know whether delivery is possible.

Delivery charges & time

  • Delivery charges depend on your location and order size, and are confirmed on WhatsApp before your order is finalised.
  • Orders in supported areas are typically delivered within about 3–5 days.
  • Bulk, festive and wedding orders may need additional preparation time, which we will confirm with you.

Pickup from our shop

You are welcome to collect your order directly from our shop in Kotkapura. Please confirm timing with us on WhatsApp before visiting.

Freshness

Products are freshly made and packed. To keep them at their best, reseal after opening and store in a cool, dry place.
